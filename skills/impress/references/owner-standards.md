# Owner standards

Load this for every substantive Impress pass on Luke Steuber's work. It is the
floor, not the target: an exemplar result meets all of it before any other
criterion counts. The task's domain skill and Luke's current request still win
where they differ.

## Access first

Accessibility is part of the core design, not a review step at the end.

- **Color vision.** Every state, status, selection, error, chart series, and
  map category stays distinguishable without hue. Pair color with text, an
  icon, a shape, a pattern, or position. Never let red versus green alone carry
  meaning. Check the result under simulated deuteranopia (the baseline),
  protanopia, tritanopia, and grayscale.
- **Input.** The primary task works with keyboard, switch access, eye gaze or
  dwell, voice, and touch where the platform supports them. Targets are large
  and stable, and nothing requires precise timing or a complex gesture.
- **Perception and cognition.** Screen readers, low vision, 200% zoom, reflow,
  reduced motion, and plain language all work. Prefer recognition over recall.

## Real, not plausible

- Visual work passes only when it is recognizably the real thing. Tests that
  pass on the wrong geometry, data, or likeness are worthless.
- Re-derive outside-model conclusions from evidence. Reproduce a defect before
  changing code.
- Search and cite dates for current information instead of answering from memory.

## Restraint

- Prefer client-side, free, and open-source options. Nothing paid,
  server-side, or tunneled unless asked.
- Give an artifact over advice, and match depth to the question.
- A correction is a spec: change only what was named.

## Words

- American English everywhere. First person singular, never "we".
- Short, plain public copy: active voice, no em dashes, no superlatives, no
  comparisons with commercial models, no all-caps labels, no middle-dot meta
  strings. Elaborate with facts, not adjectives.
- Never write "AI-powered" or use "AI" as a noun. Never name internal agents,
  skills, or tools in public content.
- Route prose through Humanize with `--profile luke` when it is installed.

## Gates that never move

Impress never authorizes publishing, store submission, deployment, or deletion.
Public wording waits for Luke's approval of the exact text.
